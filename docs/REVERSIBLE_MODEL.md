# Reversible Transformer

The reversible Transformer is intentionally deferred until the baseline is trained, resumable, evaluated, and reproducible. Its experiment will retain the tokenizer, data split, context length, parameter target, optimizer, training-token budget, seed policy, prompts, and metrics wherever possible.

The reversible block mechanism will be the primary intended architectural difference. Any unavoidable differences will be documented before comparison; no claim of improvement will be made without controlled measurements.
